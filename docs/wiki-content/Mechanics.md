## Target selection

Automatic priority: bosses, elites and Preparing/Attacking enemies → enemies approaching in the danger area → chests → other approaching enemies. Ties usually keep the current target. Manual selection must obey the same legal target restrictions.

In endless mode, enemies already permitted to attack take priority, including preparation; dangerous boss/elite charges remain selectable for focused interruption. If none qualify, living on-screen tanks with an active taunt restrict targets. Manual locks, secondaries and homing shots obey that restriction; splash, chains and penetration can still hurt others. The manual preference returns when legal. Sanctuary disables these restrictions so enemies and chests can be chosen freely. Dead/off-screen actors never keep taunt. T cycles legal targets; F reselects the current target while the arena has keyboard focus. Tapping a selected leader also reselects it.

## Damage and area effects

- Effective attack = base damage × (1 + power) × Titan multiplier; heavy volley, secondary and critical multipliers follow. Stage/boss/low-HP/slow/control bonuses add together; Judgment's charging-boss multiplier is separate.
- Secondaries start at 60% primary damage; Barrage raises this to 80% / 100%. Maximum six shots per volley, minimum {{FIRING_FLOOR}}s effective firing interval before poison penalties.
- Random critical chance caps at 85%, base multiplier 1.5. Precision tier 9 guarantees every third volley, independently of that cap.
- Each projectile remembers its victims. Penetration cannot hit one victim twice. Lightning selects distinct nearby targets at 30% triggering-shot damage, base jump distance 220.
- Splash: 15% triggering-shot damage per stack, radius 85, +20 at Storm tier 3, excluding the original victim. Storm: every 6s, radius 150, effective attack × (1 + 0.5 × at least one storm stack); family tiers improve timing and damage.
- Kill explosion: (16 + source maximum HP × 3%) × at least one fuse stack, radius 130. Blast tier 3 uses ×1.4 / radius 160; tier 9 uses ×2 / radius 190. Chains require tier 6; each death scores and heals once.
- Sacrifice requires an enemy and spends at most 3 HP, keeping at least 1. It does not consume shield or trigger invulnerability/retaliation. Damage = (20 + target maximum HP × 2%) × at least one sacrifice stack + 15% player maximum HP, rising to 45% at Blast tier 9. Interval 5s, or 4s / 3s at tiers 6 / 9.
- Thorns retaliate against all enemies for 18 × stacks on an effective ordinary hit, including shield absorption. Invulnerability, poison and sacrifice do not trigger it. Bastion tier 6 doubles thorns and adds 25% player maximum HP arena reflection; tier 9 raises reflection to 50%.
- Giant Slayer and Erosion add primary direct-hit percentage damage. Combined maximum-HP coefficient caps at 5%, current-HP at 10%. These components ignore critical/volley multipliers but retain target-state bonuses and boss reduction. Secondaries, chains, splash and counters cannot recursively trigger them. A penetrating primary can trigger once per new victim.
- Mountain Breaker uses log₂ of the enemy/player maximum-HP ratio; Godhunter applies at ≥5× player maximum HP. Entropy pulse uses each victim's maximum HP, every 4s / 3s / 2s with family tiers, capped at 5% per pulse.
- Every enemy-HP-derived component, including the added primary weak-point component, erosion pulses, explosions and sacrifice, shares a per-target allowance after modifiers: main bosses {{BOSS_PERCENT}} maximum HP/s, elites {{ELITE_PERCENT}}/s, at most 0.25s burst storage. Weakness multiplies the allowance by {{VULNERABLE_MULTIPLIER}} without resetting its stored credit. Fixed and player-HP-derived damage are unaffected; final-phase execution remains separate.
- Endless minions above one star divide received enemy-HP-derived damage by their star count, including primary percentage effects, pulses, source-HP explosions and target-HP sacrifice. From depth 7, all those enemy-HP components also share each minion’s allowance of 20% / (1 + 0.08 × (depth−7)) maximum HP per player combat second, with 0.25s storage, after modifiers and star division. The depth-20 rate is about 9.80%. Fixed damage, player-HP components and final execution keep their paths.
- Area effects, chains and retaliation cannot break chests or claim rewards. Spatial distances scale with lane spacing on desktop.

## Interrupts and weak points

Campaign and endless depths 1–3 use the displayed damage threshold or {{INTERRUPT_HITS}} primary hits during a boss/elite charge. From endless depth 4, each warning requires a fresh selection of that leader to enable the primary-hit route: tap it again, cycle onto it with T, or press F to reselect the current target. Automatic targeting, a selection before the warning and confirmation of an earlier cast do not confirm the new cast.

Confirmation is allowed during forming, but the first min(0.8s, charge duration /3) accumulates neither interruption damage nor primary-hit credit. Attacks still deal damage and trigger Winter normally. After forming, a primary counts only while that leader is both confirmed for this cast and the current target. Required hits are max(profile setting, min(12, 6 + floor((depth−1)/4))): 6/7/10 at depths 4/5/20. The damage route needs no confirmation; its threshold is at least 10% of main-boss maximum HP or 25% of elite maximum HP. Either route consumes and interrupts the cast.

Interruption opens a {{VULNERABLE_SECONDS}}s weak point: ordinary damage ×1.35, percentage allowance ×{{VULNERABLE_MULTIPLIER}}, and each primary adds a base {{WEAK_POINT_PERCENT}} target-maximum-HP component. All enemy-HP components share the same per-target bucket after modifiers. The added weak-point component grants no lifesteal; existing card-based percentage damage remains eligible, subject to the deep-endless recovery allowance.

Endless bosses and elites keep ordinary preparation and the full {{CHARGE_SECONDS}}s warning, without an additional 2.8s opening cooldown. From depth 4, their first preparation after obtaining attack permission and an already-started charge use hostile time, so slow/stun cannot stretch them. Movement and subsequent recovery retain their affected enemy clock. Pause, reward selection and Sanctuary freeze hostile time; campaign, depths 1–3 and minion preparation/charge rules remain.

An endless main boss with no queued threshold skill still charges one aimed shot for {{CHARGE_SECONDS}}s, then recovers for {{VOLLEY_RECOVERY_SECONDS}}s after a successful release. Threshold casts take priority before the next ordinary charge; active casts finish or are interrupted, and casts never overlap. Other successful threshold/elite casts use {{RECOVERY_SECONDS}}s recovery.

## Recovery, shields and wounds

Lifesteal uses actual enemy HP removed by basic attacks; overkill, execution's extra HP removal, the added weak-point component, chests, explosions, chains and counters grant none. Existing percentage-card basic damage retains its lifesteal behavior, subject to the deep-endless recovery allowance below. Sum base contributions from cards, training, families and shield bonuses, cap at 50%, then multiply by 0.85: effective cap 42.5%. Base 4% becomes 3.4%; base 2% becomes 1.7%.

From endless depth 4, lifesteal first spends a separate recovery allowance: player maximum HP × effective lifesteal ×0.25 per player combat second, storing at most 0.25s. Healing bonuses, wounds and overheal conversion apply afterward; other healing does not spend this lifesteal-specific allowance. From depth 7, the shared combat recovery allowance below also applies. Pause, reward selection and visual-only clearance do not refill it. Sanctuary keeps player time running, so refill continues. Changing lifesteal or maximum HP updates capacity without granting a fresh full allowance; entry/restart resets to at most one quarter-second allowance. Campaign and depths 1–3 remain unrestricted.

From endless depth 7, actual combat healing and generated shields share player maximum HP ×0.04 / (1 + 0.075 × (depth−7)) per player combat second, with 0.25s storage: 4% at depth 7 and about 2.03% at depth 20. Apply healing bonuses and wounds before charging actual gains, in the original source-call order. Each heal settles its missing-HP portion first; only true overhealing converts, and budget-denied healing is not overflow. Converted shield spends the allowance once. Lifesteal still uses its earlier independent base-healing allowance. Instant card effects, maximum-HP increases and inter-stage healing retain their original rules. Pause, rewards and clearance do not refill the shared allowance; Sanctuary does. Stat changes clip capacity without minting credit; stage entry/restart grants at most a fresh quarter-second allowance.

Future combat shield generation is also limited to the smaller of its original capacity and player maximum HP ×max(0.25, 1 −0.05 ×(depth−6)): 95% at depth 7, 30% at depth 20, with a 25% floor. Existing excess shields remain; immediate card shields keep their path. Build displays the shared recovery rate and combat shield ceiling.

Combat lifesteal, kill healing and regeneration receive the healing bonus. Instant card healing uses its listed amount. **Wounds reduce all healing, regeneration and shield gains by 90%, leaving 10%.** A 30 heal becomes 3; 24 shield becomes 2.4; maximum-HP increases are unaffected. Poison immediately applies wounds even if shield absorbs every poison tick. Reapplication extends remaining time by 3s, without increasing the 90% penalty.

Overheal conversion uses already-reduced combat healing, without applying wounds a second time. Overflow adds 10% per stack; Vessel adds 5 points; Blood tiers 6 / 9 add 50% / 100%, total capped at 100%. Base storage is 20% maximum HP, Blood raises it to 40% / 80%, Vessel adds 10 points per stack, final cap 100%. Existing directly granted shield above this storage cap is kept. Shielded Siphon requires positive shield. Instant card and inter-stage healing do not convert. Direct, Bastion and Dominion shield gains all receive wounds. Stage transitions cleanse before healing up to 35 HP.

## Poison and attack-speed penalties

Wizard actions choose poison or a shot; shooting and death do not apply poison. Poison lasts 3s, starts at 10 HP/s, drains shield first, bypasses ordinary hit invulnerability and does not trigger per-frame counters. Each repeat adds 3s and independently chooses +1 HP/s or +1 percentage point of total attack-speed reduction. Escalation persists within that poison instance and clears on expiry/cleanse. Enemy scaling, stars, summons and stage multipliers do not scale poison.

While poisoned without shield, total attack-speed reduction starts at 30%, capped at 90%. Final interval divides by 0.7 at 30%, 0.69 at 31%. Positive shield immediately suppresses the penalty; losing shield restores it while poison lasts. Sanctuary clears poison, wounds and escalation. HUD displays remaining time, current poison damage and penalties.

## Sanctuary: cleansing and hostile-time freeze

Silent Dominion triggers every 8s of hostile time, or 6s at Dominion tier 6, which can trigger it without the card. Duration = (0.5 + 0.25 × enhancement stacks) × Dominion duration multiplier: 1.3 at tiers 3 / 6, 1.6 at tier 9. Silent Dominion adds one enhancement stack; Chronocrown adds two, separately from family stacks. One Silent Dominion gives 0.75s, one Chronocrown gives 1s; four Silent Dominion plus Chronocrown at tier 9 gives 3.2s. Bosses receive full duration.

Activation cleanses before that step's damage. Freeze enemy movement, preparation, attacks, charging, reduction timers, hostile projectile movement/lifetime/collision, spawns, escorts, chest movement, supplies, stage clock, fusion and enemy skills. Player fire, friendly shots, healing, build effects and cumulative survival time continue. Taunt is disabled. Boss HP-threshold skills wait until Sanctuary ends; killing the boss cancels them. Dominion tier 6 also clears hostile bullets; lower tiers only freeze them. Sanctuary does not recharge during itself. Pause/rewards preserve its remainder; stage/reset clears it.

## Ordinary slow and stun

Slow affects movement, preparation, attacks and campaign charge timers, capped at 70%; main bosses receive half strength. Repeated slow keeps the stronger intensity and longer remaining duration. Stun freezes these actions: main bosses retain quarter-duration stuns/freezes, elites retain full duration, and Dominion scaling still applies. Campaign enemies and ordinary endless minions keep the existing longer-remaining-stun refresh rule. Two/three-star endless minions divide newly applied slow strength and stun duration by two/three. Slow duration and Winter’s all-basic-projectile hit counting remain; fusion does not forcibly clear an existing stronger effect.

An endless boss or elite accepts one effective stun, then rejects further stuns/freezes during that stun and for {{LEADER_STUN_RECOVERY_SECONDS}}s afterward. Even a longer incoming stun cannot refresh it. The recovery timer uses hostile time and freezes with pause, reward selection and Sanctuary. Damage, slow and cast interruption remain available. From depth 4, ordinary slow/stun cannot delay the first permitted preparation or an active charge, while movement and later recovery still use the affected enemy clock. Pause and Sanctuary still freeze the hostile clock. Cast interruption follows its own forming and current-cast confirmation rules. Winter still counts all basic-projectile hits; Dread Sigil counts every fourth primary hit on one enemy. Shatter/control bonuses recognize their corresponding states, including preparation, charge and Sanctuary where applicable.

## Rewards and epics

`campaignChestTiming(index, bossAt)` distributes campaign chest opportunities. Short authored waves keep the first chest at {{CHEST_FIRST}}s, {{CHEST_BASE_INTERVAL}}s intervals and the {{CHEST_BASE_LAST}}s cutoff, also capped five seconds before the boss. A longer pre-boss wave spreads the same authored number of opportunities through five seconds before its new boss arrival, without increasing total card opportunities. Current four-stage planned counts are {{CAMPAIGN_CHEST_COUNTS}}; actual collection depends on clearing the active chest. Offers contain distinct eligible cards below cap. Before the main family reaches nine stacks, guided offers prefer two main-family choices while retaining foundational offense. At nine, slots become continuation / fixed complement / free, falling back to remaining eligible cards if needed. A legal foundation upgrade remains available after nine stacks as well, and epic promotion preserves the only foundation option. Complement pairs are Barrage↔Precision, Might↔Giant Slayer, Blast↔Erosion, Blood↔Bastion, Frost↔Dominion and Judgment↔Storm.

A final guided offer has at most one epic: 10%/15%/20%/25% in campaign stages 1–4, 30% in endless. There is no three-card acquisition gate. The single boss augment slot rolls rarity directly at the same rate, rather than taking one of three candidates and diluting it. With no eligible epic, use a common/rare candidate without banking a future promotion. Epics remain once per run. Stage one allows only Barrage, Might, Precision and Storm, including epics.

Full-stage rewards contain two distinct trainings and one augment: choose one. The fourth reward precedes victory. Training adds no family stack, and maximum-HP training does not heal. Automatic training uses **separate training credit**, with cumulative threshold T(n)={{TRAINING_BASE}}n+{{TRAINING_STEP}}n(n−1)/2: 5,000/11,500/19,500/29,000/40,000 for the first five. Marginal requirements rise arithmetically, not exponentially. Only finite-budget kills add training credit; clearance HP bonuses and excess reinforcements add display score only. Fusion preserves and sums existing credit. Manual training does not increase automatic threshold count.

Automatic training neither pauses nor spends score; multiple crossed thresholds settle once each. Stage one excludes lifesteal; effective capped crit/lifesteal/haste options leave the pool. Every reward has a one-use token. The official endless profile grants a supply after each of its first three combat segments and one mixed final reward; custom totals follow cumulative floor(N × k / 3) across those three boundaries; the paired elite segment adds no additional supply.

## Pause, endings and restart

Manual pause, backgrounding and blur stop combat; return requires manual resume. Pause, selection and endings freeze all combat/status time. Sanctuary counts toward cumulative survival but not stage time. Main-boss death stops skill summons and recurring escorts; unfinished finite initial escort allocations must still enter and clear, with existing minions and bullets active. Complete cleanup clears dangerous shots and retains 0.8s visual afterglow before the reward; all combat, statuses and both clocks stop during it. Death echoes last 0.5/0.6/0.8s for minions/elites/bosses, release their combat slot immediately and never re-trigger rewards. Until cleanup, remaining enemies can still defeat you. No stage timeout exists. Zero player HP loses. Restart resets health, firepower, cards, training, synergies, actors, shots, statuses, targets, rewards, clocks, attack cells and score. There are no accounts or combat saves.
